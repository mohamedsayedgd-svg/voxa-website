03 / ABOUT VOXA

Born from
real 3D work.

VOXA is an independent software initiative founded by Mohamed Sayed, a 3D design professional with hands-on experience in production workflows. The project explores tools for modeling efficiency, repeatable tasks, and a smoother creative process within Autodesk 3ds Max.

Our current focus is developing and refining the first utilities while building the foundation for a broader tool family.
